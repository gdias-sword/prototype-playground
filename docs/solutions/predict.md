# Predict

## Predict Overview

Sword Predict is an innovative end-to-end solution designed to detect and prevent high-cost events — including MSK surgeries and pelvic health procedures — before they occur. Using advanced machine learning algorithms, Predict identifies members who are on track to be the highest cost claimants for MSK and pelvic health more than 6 months before they occur (on average), enabling the highest savings and best member outcomes at no increased member cost.

Key features and benefits of Predict include:

- **Early Detection:** Predict uses AI to analyze medical claims and eligibility data, identifying at-risk members well in advance of potential high-cost events.
- **Proactive Engagement:** Once high-risk members are identified, Predict powers personalized content delivery to ensure timely intervention.
- **Concierge-Level Support:** Predict provides a dedicated concierge team to guide high-risk members through the enrollment process. This personalized assistance ensures that members not only enroll but also complete their tailored programs, maximizing the impact of Sword interventions.
- **Optimized Treatment:** Predict seamlessly integrates with all Sword treatment verticals, such as Thrive and Bloom, ensuring members receive the most appropriate care.
- **Impressive Results:** In a case study with a 28,000-life client, Predict increased high-risk member enrollment in Sword by 500%, resulting in an additional $2.8M in annualized savings — a 50% increase in overall program yield. Members who enrolled through Predict demonstrate significantly higher ROI at $4,402 per member, 50% higher than the average member who self-enrolls.
- **Efficient Communication:** Using personalized messaging, Predict can enroll high-risk members using 80% fewer touchpoints than broad-based marketing.
- **DE&I Impact:** Predict helps address health equity by identifying and engaging underserved populations, including more Black members, members without college degrees, and those with higher obesity rates and pain severity.

**Predict's impact on the business:**

- Ensures the highest possible ROI by enrolling members before they become high cost
- Engages members who can benefit the most from Sword programs, maximizing the impact of interventions
- Focuses on the 4% of members who drive 80% of costs, enabling outsized savings and better health outcomes
- Integrates with client's existing solutions to support broader ecosystem integration
- Delivers enhanced outcomes without increasing the per-member price, making it a highly valuable addition to Sword's offerings

Predict is currently available in the U.S. and can be implemented for employers of all sizes, making it a versatile and powerful tool in our suite of digital health solutions.

## Key Capabilities and Business Impact

Sword Predict is an industry-first, AI-powered predictive marketing and clinical intelligence solution designed to identify and engage members before high-cost musculoskeletal (MSK) and pelvic health events occur. Unlike retrospective claims analysis, Predict proactively identifies the fewer than 5% of members who are projected to drive more than 50% of MSK-related costs in the next 6 to 9 months. This allows for earlier intervention, better outcomes, and significantly reduced spend.

**Key capabilities of Predict include:**

- **Early Risk Identification:** Predict analyzes medical and pharmacy claims, prior authorization data, eligibility files, and demographics to detect members at risk of high-cost interventions such as surgery or advanced MSK deterioration.
- **Proactive, Personalized Engagement:** A dedicated concierge team delivers custom outreach and tailored marketing to enroll at-risk members into appropriate Sword programs.
- **End-to-End Integration:** Predict integrates across all Sword care verticals, including Thrive (MSK pain), Bloom (pelvic health), and Pulse (cardiometabolic health), ensuring that high-risk members receive the right care at the right time.
- **Demonstrated Outcomes:** In a 28,000-member client population, Predict increased enrollment among high-risk members by 500%, driving an additional $2.8M in annualized savings and improving ROI to $4,402 per enrolled member — 50% higher than standard self-enrollment.
- **Health Equity Impact:** Predict enables earlier engagement for underserved populations, including higher rates of engagement among Black members, those without college degrees, and individuals with elevated obesity and pain severity.

**Predict's business impact:**

- Maximizes ROI by enrolling members before they become high-cost
- Focuses on the 4% of members who drive 80% of MSK costs
- Reduces reliance on broad, inefficient outreach (80% fewer touchpoints)
- Seamlessly integrates with employer or health plan ecosystems
- Delivers higher savings and better outcomes at no additional per-member cost
