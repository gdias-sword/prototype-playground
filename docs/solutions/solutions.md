# Solutions

## What Kinds of User Input Were Used During the Design Process?

From the beginning, Sword developed its solutions with the member at the center. As we continue to develop and improve our offerings, we continue to bring the member's voice into the process to ensure a highly personalized and intuitive experience.

Sword was initially built through extensive discussion, problem-solving sessions, and testing with patients, providers, physical therapists, engineers, and other key stakeholders to identify member preferences and relevant behavior and validate clinical safety, functionality, and the therapeutic value of digital interactions.

As members engage with Sword, we collect input from objective session data, subjective session ratings, member feedback to their DPT, user experience research (UXR, e.g., surveys, interviews, focus groups), DPT feedback and more to understand the experience. With many changes, we use formal testing procedures to identify how members respond and the impact a change has on their experiences and outcomes. The feedback collected influences our product roadmap and helps identify priorities not only based on what members say but also on how they respond.

Additionally, we've collected input from members during clinical trials by comparing Sword to in-person physical therapy, not only showing efficacy and acceptance but also finding a higher level of engagement with our solution. One key insight was the value of real-time feedback and human-like interaction during therapy sessions, which led to the development of Sword Phoenix, the AI engine that powers intelligent movement correction and conversational guidance. Ultimately, user input drove Sword to prioritize simplicity, personalization, and clinical quality, resulting in a platform that balances advanced technology with human care.

## Programs / Solutions Brief Overview

- **Thrive** — AI-driven Musculoskeletal Care (MSK) managed by Doctors of Physical Therapy (DPTs), including acute, chronic, post-surgical, and prevention-focused care.
- **Bloom** — AI-driven Women's Health (Pelvic & Menopause Care) managed by specialists (DPTs specialising in pelvic health, and — starting mid-2026 — nurses providing holistic women's health support).
- **Mind** — AI-driven mental health care integrating licensed Ph.D.-level psychologists with digital engagement tools.
- **Pulse** — AI-driven care for cardiometabolic conditions, integrating exercise, nutrition and lifestyle interventions, managed by Personal Health Specialists trained as DPTs, as well as nurses for comprehensive care.

These care programs are made available to the entire population, and are further enhanced through Predictive Analytics & Engagement Services (**Predict**), which enables data-driven identification and targeted outreach to high-risk members to support appropriate clinical intervention.

## Programs / Solutions Detailed Overview

Sword Health delivers innovative, AI-driven care solutions designed to address musculoskeletal (MSK), women's health, cardiometabolic health, and mental health needs, combining clinical expertise with advanced technology.

**Thrive:** Comprehensive care for acute and chronic musculoskeletal conditions, post-surgical rehabilitation, and injury prevention. Thrive provides AI MSK care by pairing AI-powered technology with licensed Doctors of Physical Therapy (DPTs) to deliver clinical-grade outcomes at home. The program uses movement capture technology to provide real-time feedback during therapy sessions and generate actionable data for the DPT. Members also have access to the Sword mobile app, where they can complete sessions, track progress, access educational content, and communicate with their clinician.

**Bloom:** A specialized women's health platform designed to support pelvic health and key life stages such as pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and menopause. Care is delivered by Pelvic Health Specialists (Doctors of Physical Therapy with specialized pelvic health training) who guide members through personalized therapy programs. When clinically appropriate, members may receive an FDA-listed intravaginal biofeedback pod that measures pelvic floor muscle activity and provides real-time feedback during exercises. Members can also access the Sword app to complete sessions, track progress, and communicate with their clinician.

**Pulse:** An AI-driven cardiometabolic health solution designed to support members managing or at risk for conditions such as hypertension, pre-diabetes, and obesity, including support for individuals using GLP-1 medications. Pulse focuses on addressing root causes through personalized programs that combine movement, nutrition, behavioral health support, and continuous monitoring, helping members build sustainable lifestyle changes that improve long-term health outcomes.

**Mind:** An AI-powered mental health solution that provides continuous support for members experiencing stress, anxiety, depression, and other mental health challenges. Mind combines AI-driven guidance with oversight from licensed PhD-level mental health specialists, delivering personalized support and evidence-based techniques to help members improve their mental wellbeing.

**Academy:** Sword's educational content library, available through the mobile app, which provides expert-led videos and resources focused on pain science, movement, ergonomics, and overall wellness to support members throughout their care journey.

**Predict:** An AI-powered predictive analytics solution that identifies members at high risk of incurring costly healthcare interventions. By proactively identifying and engaging these individuals earlier, Predict helps guide them to the most appropriate clinical program, improving outcomes while reducing avoidable healthcare costs.
