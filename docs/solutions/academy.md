# Academy

## Academy Overview

Sword also offers its internal knowledge base to all members — the Academy — which provides premium original content for educational awareness. Sword has developed a variety of videos through clinical research and guidance from our Doctors of Physical Therapy to directly address a full suite of pain issues. These videos demonstrate proper movements, stretches, posture, and more that will empower users to avoid injuries through everyday best practices in physical health.

## Educational Content

Sword Health offers a variety of educational content to its members through the Academy, including clinically validated, easy-to-understand content to help improve health outcomes. This catalog consists of 50+ series composed of individual episodes, and over 70 short standalone videos, making up a total of ~300 videos on dozens of topics — from joint pain and headaches to women's health, sleep and mental wellness — featuring leading specialists in their field. All members (enrolled or not in a program) have access to Academy.

We offer Academy at no additional cost because clinically validated education is a proven driver of behavioral change — and ultimately, better health outcomes. Sword has a dedicated team focused on delivering and planning new Academy content to keep content current and engaging. While we regularly receive member feedback on topics of interest, we typically do not support client-specific content customization. This allows our clinical and educational experts to prioritize the topics most relevant to our broader member base, based on evolving needs and market trends.

## Academy with 3-Way Chat

### Are Educational Resources Proactively Delivered, or Only on Request?

Academy resources are not limited to on-demand requests. Phoenix is designed to proactively share relevant educational content and resources with members at appropriate moments. While members may not have the ability to freely browse the content library, they are always encouraged to specifically request resources from Phoenix on any topic.
