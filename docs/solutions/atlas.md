# Atlas

## Atlas Overview

Data regarding the global epidemic of pain is clear. On average, employees outside of the U.S. will be absent from work for more than 14 days per year due to MSK-related issues. When an individual begins the process of addressing this, they often encounter the same problem: direct access to a physical therapist is not available in ~75% of countries around the world, which means options are very limited. As an industry-leading digital physical therapy provider, Sword developed the technology needed to help.

Atlas is the world's first app-based solution designed specifically to provide access to exercises and educational content aimed at targeting and reducing member pain from anywhere around the world. With Atlas we are able to advance health equity by combining clinically validated exercises with premium educational content to help members address their acute and chronic pain.

The impact of this can be dramatic, for both the member and the employer, in a number of important areas:

- Decrease in healthcare costs
- Increase in workforce productivity
- Reduction of absenteeism, pain, and injuries

We created Atlas as an alternative for members who are living with pain, but may be enrolled in a therapeutic program that is too challenging to follow, inconvenient to access, or difficult to maintain. Atlas boasts an easy-to-use interface that is complete with well-designed workout programs to empower any member to participate.

Attributes that set Atlas apart include:

- **Global Accessibility:** Access to the Sword Mobile App is available in 150 countries, which means participants will be able to workout and find relief from pain, regardless of where they live.
- **Evidence-Based Exercises:** Exercises in Atlas have been developed by Doctors of Physical Therapy, and designed specifically to address acute and chronic pain while building strength and mobility.
- **Premium Educational Resources:** Members have access to a full library of scientifically backed video content that keeps them informed, motivated, and pain-free, all available from their smartphone.
