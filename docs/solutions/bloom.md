# Bloom

## Bloom Overview

Bloom is Sword's AI-powered women's health solution. It provides AI Care for pelvic health, menopause, pregnancy recovery, and fertility. Phoenix, Sword's AI care agent, leads every interaction through text and voice, delivering therapeutic exercises, CBT, mindfulness, nutrition, and education — all personalized in real time. Clinicians remain in the loop for oversight and complex cases that require human judgment.

Bloom aims to meet women's health needs where traditional healthcare has fallen short. The program supports members experiencing:

- Urinary incontinence
- Pelvic pain and pressure
- Bowel dysfunction
- Pain with intercourse
- Diastasis recti
- Pregnancy-related musculoskeletal pain
- Postpartum recovery concerns

Bloom integrates licensed clinical oversight with biofeedback-enabled exercise therapy delivered through the Sword mobile application.

### Clinical Model

Members are paired 1:1 with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy who has specialized training in women's health. Clinical responsibilities include:

- Comprehensive intake and assessment
- Identification of comorbid musculoskeletal factors
- Development of an individualized care plan
- Ongoing monitoring and progression
- Symptom tracking and reassessment

Pelvic health conditions frequently coexist with abdominal, hip, and low back dysfunction. Care plans address these contributing musculoskeletal factors when clinically appropriate. Escalation to in-network providers or specialists occurs when symptoms fall outside digital scope or require in-person evaluation.

### Technology and Biofeedback

Bloom includes the Bloom Pod, an FDA-listed intravaginal pressure sensor designed to measure:

- Contraction strength
- Endurance
- Relaxation control
- Muscle elongation

The device connects securely to the Sword mobile application and provides real-time feedback during exercise sessions. Biofeedback data is transmitted to the assigned clinician, enabling:

- Objective performance tracking
- Precision in exercise progression
- Adjustment based on measurable response

The device is constructed of medical-grade silicone and designed for safe, discreet use.

### Education and Behavioral Support

Members have access to pelvic health education modules within the application, including:

- Anatomy and pain education
- Lifestyle modification guidance
- Recovery expectations
- Cognitive behavioral strategies when appropriate

Educational content is developed and reviewed by clinical experts.

### Outcomes and Engagement

Bloom applies the same structured reassessment cadence used in Sword's broader MSK programs, including validated outcome measures and functional improvement tracking. Clinical oversight ensures measurable progress and safe advancement.

### Eligibility and Access

Bloom is delivered through the Sword mobile application and Bloom Pod. No additional hardware is required. Members may engage in live video sessions, secure messaging, and asynchronous guidance with their assigned clinician.

### Future State Vision & Planned Enhancements

The new Bloom is defined by several structural advancements:

**1. From "Condition-First" to "Life Stage" Scope**

The existing program is a pelvic-first solution focused on specific conditions such as leakage or pain. The new platform expands to cover the full continuum of women's health, organizing care around real-world life stages including:

- Fertility
- Pregnancy
- Postpartum
- Menopause

Members transition across these stages without losing clinical context or restarting their journey. Care evolves with the member rather than resetting around isolated diagnoses.

**2. The Evolution of Clinical Memory**

In the existing program, context may not fully persist across separate care pathways. The new platform establishes Clinical Memory, powered by Phoenix, as a core system capability. Clinical Memory retains longitudinal data including:

- What interventions were effective
- What was not effective
- External care received
- Historical symptom patterns

This allows each subsequent plan of care to become more precise and personalized over time, rather than repetitive or episodic.

**3. Biometric Signals Versus Static Check-Ins**

The platform is evolving from a reliance on periodic self-reporting toward a real-time physiological input layer. By integrating wearable-derived data such as sleep, stress proxies, and cycle patterns, the system can identify physiological drift earlier and adapt the care plan based on objective signals, not solely subjective reporting.

## Who Receives the Bloom Pod?

In the U.S., all members — excluding those who are pregnant — receive a Bloom Kit, which includes a pod: an intravaginal device that tracks pelvic floor muscle contractions and relaxations, providing real-time feedback and results through the app. Additionally, members have access to sessions without the pod via the app, providing an alternative for times when a member may need or want to perform a session without the pod.

## Call with Pelvic Health Specialist (PHS)

Every member has the opportunity to schedule a call with a Pelvic Health Specialist (PHS) who holds a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree. Members can schedule a call for as soon as an hour after completing enrollment, with the average time to call around 3 days. Approximately 93% of Bloom members schedule a call at their initial onboarding.

---

## Pelvic Health Specialist (PHS)

Each member is matched with a dedicated Pelvic Health Specialist (PHS), a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) trained in pelvic health, who provides personalized, evidence-based treatment plans.

---

## Recommended Number of Sessions

For Bloom, the recommendation is 3 sessions per week. The program duration varies based on clinical outcomes and individual progress. A program may include three sessions per week over ten weeks, but this will vary based on the patient's goals and condition.

---

## Session Data Shared with PHS

The results are shared with the dedicated PHS. Since sessions are performed with an FDA-listed device, not only does the PHS see subjective feedback from the member (e.g., how they felt during the session), they can see objective data for each repetition of each movement. This allows the PHS to tailor the experience for the specific member, identifying areas where the member needs additional support and making changes to keep the program appropriate and engaging.

---

## Typical Bloom Exercises

The program includes exercises to improve pelvic floor muscle coordination, strength, and endurance. The Bloom program goes beyond exercises centered solely on contractions or Kegels by offering curated exercises involving both contraction and relaxation of the pelvic floor muscles.

Two main categories of exercise are used:

- **Isolated exercises** — work on the contraction and relaxation of the pelvic floor, including exercises focused on contraction strength, sustained holds, agility, and control.
- **Coordinated or functional exercises** — ensure that the pelvic floor muscles are integrated seamlessly into everyday movements and activities, enhancing day-to-day function. These include movements like bridges, pelvic tilts, and diaphragmatic breathing.

---

## Session Feedback and Member Progress Tracking

During the session, the sensor enables our AI system (Phoenix) to provide instant feedback on exercise performance. Members see visual and hear audio cues and are guided through changes to correct their performance. Members can track their progress within the Sword app, seeing detailed session overviews as well as summarized and gamified (e.g., badges) progress information.

This performance data is also shared with the assigned PHS, who analyzes the results and provides post-session check-ins and feedback. The PHS can also make adjustments to the program at any time during its course to better suit the patient and ensure continuous improvement.

## Program for Pregnant Members

Bloom includes a pregnancy-specific program that addresses the needs of this specific population from the first trimester through birth. The Academy includes a variety of content related to various common pregnancy concerns, providing clinical insights to further the understanding and management of pelvic health throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery.

---

## How Soon After Childbirth Can Members Begin Bloom?

Members may enroll as soon as they feel ready, but it is not advised to use the pod until they have been "cleared" by their medical provider, which generally occurs at around 6 weeks postpartum.
