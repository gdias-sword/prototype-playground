# Mind

## Mind as a Key Differentiator of Sword

A key differentiator of Sword is our ability to deliver truly whole-person care by integrating mental health directly into our MSK programs. Our physical therapists are trained to identify signs of emotional distress — like stress, anxiety, or burnout — during MSK treatment and can initiate warm handovers to licensed Ph.D.-level psychologists via our new mental health solution, Mind. This ensures members receive support at the exact moment they need it, not weeks later or not at all.

Mind moves beyond episodic talk therapy, offering a proactive, always-on model that combines human care with AI. Members engage daily with licensed psychologists, supported by our AI therapist Phoenix and insights from the M-band wearable. The M-band captures mood, sleep, and movement data, enabling specialists to create highly personalized care plans that adapt in real time. This integrated model removes access barriers, increases engagement, and delivers measurable outcomes, while employers only pay when members improve.

## Mind Overview

Mind is an always-on mental health program that delivers proactive, continuous and personalized support at scale. Unlike traditional session-based therapy models, Mind provides daily, in-the-moment care through a psychologist-governed AI care model, combining continuous engagement, real-time personalization, and outcomes-aligned pricing to deliver measurable clinical and financial impact for employers.

## What Problem in the Current Mental Health Care System Is Mind Designed to Solve?

Mental healthcare was never designed to keep people well. It was designed to intervene when they break. Mind rewires that equation — replacing the bottleneck of therapist availability with 24/7 AI-powered support that moves mental healthcare from crisis management to continuous care.

Additionally, Mind aligns incentives through outcomes-based pricing: with $0 PEPM and fees earned only when members engage and get better, Mind reduces financial risk and ensures employer investment is tied to measurable results.

## What Are the Main Components of Mind?

Three breakthrough innovations power Mind:

- **Phoenix, Sword's AI Care agent:** Purpose-built by Sword's Frontier AI lab to address mental health conditions in a comprehensive, tailored, and holistic way.
- **Mind clinicians:** Ph.D.-level mental health professionals work in concert with Phoenix to continuously engage with patients daily, ensuring the human touch remains central to care.
- **M-band:** A proprietary wearable device that captures the patient's full environmental and physiological context, to enable context-aware care and proactive outreach.

---

## How Does Mind Work?

Each member is supported by a dynamic trio: a licensed clinician, Phoenix AI, and the M-band wearable that tracks key signals 24/7. Members enroll through a mobile app and begin with a clinical intake conducted by a licensed psychologist, to assess fit, understand goals, and develop a personalized plan. They then receive personalized, ongoing support through structured, evidence-based activities and continuous chat interaction with Phoenix. Activities focus on cognitive skills, behavioral activation, sleep, stress regulation, and functional improvement.

In addition to asynchronous support, members can also participate in therapy sessions with their assigned licensed psychologist as part of their care. The program is adaptive over time, with support intensity adjusted based on member needs and progress.

## How Does Mind Differentiate from Traditional Solutions?

Traditional therapy typically relies on periodic 45-minute sessions that capture only brief snapshots of a person's experience. Mind instead delivers continuous, always-on care embedded into daily life, helping members translate therapeutic concepts into everyday actions.

Key differences include:

- **Continuous support:** Members engage through ongoing interactions and guided daily activities, not just scheduled sessions.
- **Context-aware care:** Support adapts in real time using signals such as wearable biometric data, calendar context, and engagement patterns.
- **Outcomes-based value:** Employers pay for engagement and outcomes rather than sessions, with real-time visibility into impact.

## How Are You Different from Other Mental Health Chatbots like Ash by Slingshot?

Chatbots provide conversation; Mind combines an AI Care agent with a clinician in the loop to ensure safety and results. Phoenix isn't a chatbot — it's an AI Care agent that delivers psychologist-defined interventions within strict clinical guardrails and under continuous clinician oversight. It does not diagnose, and defers to the clinician for clinical decisions. This ensures both safety and efficacy — something consumer chatbots can't guarantee.
