# Thrive

## Thrive Overview

Sword Health offers Thrive, one of the most clinically rigorous AI MSK care programs available today, designed for individuals experiencing acute or chronic musculoskeletal pain, recovering from surgery, or seeking to prevent future injuries. Every member is paired with a dedicated Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and supported by Sword's advanced digital technology, including FDA-listed medical devices and proprietary computer vision technology that guide members through their recovery journey.

During each therapy session, Sword's technology analyzes movement in real time and provides immediate corrective feedback, helping members perform exercises safely and effectively. At the same time, the member's DPT monitors progress, reviews session data, and continuously adjusts the care plan to ensure optimal outcomes and a highly personalized experience.

Thrive is a comprehensive AI MSK care solution covering conditions across the entire body, including low back, knee, hip, shoulder, neck, wrist, ankle, and elbow. Programs are tailored to support acute injuries, chronic conditions, post-surgical rehabilitation, and proactive musculoskeletal health, ensuring members receive the right level of care at every stage of their recovery journey.

Clinical guidelines for musculoskeletal pain consistently emphasize therapeutic exercise combined with education and behavioral support as the most effective treatment approach. Thrive incorporates these evidence-based principles by combining personalized exercise therapy with educational resources and behavioral coaching designed to help members better understand their pain and build healthier movement habits.

To reinforce these principles, members also have access to Sword Academy, a library of short educational videos from clinical experts that help members better understand pain, reduce injury risk, and maintain long-term musculoskeletal health.

Finally, Thrive includes mindfulness and relaxation exercises developed by mental health specialists, which can be particularly beneficial for members experiencing chronic pain or related symptoms such as anxiety or stress.

## Technologies Thrive Uses

In Sword Thrive, members receive our FDA-listed device (the Thrive Pad) for their tailored program, when they do not have a compatible device. The Thrive Pad is a purpose-built device optimized for capturing movement. Throughout sessions, members receive real-time feedback to enable safe, effective performance. The movement data — which is captured for every repetition of every exercise — is shared with the member's physical therapist (PT), enabling the PT to adjust the program, educate the member, and deliver outcomes.

## Specific Conditions Addressed

Thrive is designed to treat a wide range of musculoskeletal (MSK) conditions affecting the spine and joints across the body. Through personalized care plans created and monitored by a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT), Thrive supports members experiencing both acute injuries and chronic pain, as well as those recovering from surgery or seeking to improve mobility and prevent future injury.

Common conditions addressed through Thrive include:

- **Spine conditions:** low back pain, neck pain, herniated discs, sciatica, radiculopathy, and spinal stenosis
- **Joint and soft tissue conditions:** arthritis, tendinopathy, bursitis, and joint instability
- **Upper extremity conditions:** shoulder impingement, rotator cuff injuries, tennis elbow, golfer's elbow, wrist and hand pain
- **Lower extremity conditions:** knee pain, patellofemoral syndrome, ligament sprains, hip pain, ankle sprains, and Achilles tendinopathy
- **Post-surgical rehabilitation:** recovery following procedures such as knee arthroscopy, rotator cuff repair, or joint replacement
- **Movement dysfunction and mobility limitations:** reduced range of motion, muscle weakness, and biomechanical imbalances contributing to pain

By addressing the root causes of pain through therapeutic exercise, education, and behavioral support, Thrive helps members improve function, restore mobility, and reduce the likelihood of future injuries.

## Pain Management & Prescriptions

Sword has more clinical papers published on the impact of digital MSK on reducing pain medication utilization than any other vendor.

Reducing opioid utilization is an important part of our program and a reason we've opted to take the same stance against TENS as that of the military.

Our approach to pain management focuses on proactive, expert-led care, which includes exercise, education, and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) to tackle the root cause of MSK pain. The goal is to reduce pain and disability through effective Physical Therapy. This approach has resulted in significant reductions in pain, intent to surgery, and opioid use.

Medications are reviewed for every member during the onboarding process and initial assessment with their Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT). All Sword DPTs receive 6–7 years of post-secondary education and study the same coursework in undergrad school as medical doctors and pharmacists. A large part of their curriculum is to identify the use and abuse of opioids and pain medications and deploy non-pharmacological pain management techniques and education to help individuals address their pain without these substances.

Key results:

- **Elbow pain:** Reduction in pain medication usage from 33% at baseline to 15.7% by end of study (07/2022)
- **Low back pain:** Reduction in pain medication usage from 35.7% at baseline to 10.8% by end of study (04/2022)
- **Shoulder pain:** 44% of participants stopped usage of any pain medication by end of study (03/2022)
- **Age-based analysis:** Pain medication utilization was highest in older adults (34.3%) vs. middle-aged adults (27.1%) vs. younger adults (16%) at baseline; however, significant reductions in utilization were observed across all age groups by the end of the study (08/2023)

## Intake Process

Sword Health's intake process begins with members enrolling through a dedicated landing page, where they complete a questionnaire to identify their specific pain points and check for disqualifying criteria. This is followed by a consultation with a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) who conducts a comprehensive onboarding and thorough assessment for any red flags.

The full onboarding process takes about five minutes to complete online and allows us to collect the necessary information — such as basic characteristics of their condition and location information so we can ship the kit and communicate with the member. This allows them to self-select the Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) they would like to work with and schedule their first virtual assessment call. We accept everyone into the program who is in need. There are no groups or cycles of acceptance — as members need the program, they can access it at any time, enroll, and begin after completing their first virtual assessment call.

On the video assessment call, the DPT asks more detailed questions about the member's pain and condition, when and how it started, what has historically relieved pain (if anything), and goals of care — all of which allows them to drill down to the root cause of the disorder. They then develop a personalized care plan based on all of this information and remotely upload it to the member's Sword profile, at which point the member is officially in the program.

While any individual suffering from musculoskeletal (MSK) pain has the potential to benefit from our program, there are certain eligibility criteria that need to be met. These include:

- Being over 13 years of age
- Being able to perform approximately 20 minutes of light to moderate physical activity
- Not having recent onset fever, chills, visible inflammation in the affected area, signs of progressive neurological issues in the area of pain, an active cancer or receiving treatment for cancer, or pain related to significant trauma to that area

These exclusion criteria are screened for during the onboarding process.
